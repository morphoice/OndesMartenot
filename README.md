Maurice Martenot's 1928 electronic wail, squeezed into one Faust file.

A sine core you glide along the **ruban**. The **touche** sets how hard you lean in. A drawer of timbres runs from pure *onde* to breathy *souffle*. Then come the four diffuseurs. One is a plain loudspeaker. One is a palm of twelve strings that hum along whether you asked them or not. One is a gong pretending to be a speaker cone. One is a spring that chirps back at you.

Paste `ondes.dsp` into the [Faust IDE](https://faustide.grame.fr) and play it. No Messiaen required.
